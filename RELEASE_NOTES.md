# TV Prompter release notes

## 1.0.0: 2026-10-24

This is the first public version.

### What's in it
- Your notes in big white text on a black screen, scrolling as slowly as you like. There are 12 text sizes and 18 speeds (the slowest one barely moves).
- An edit page on your home network. Actually, the TV shows its address at the bottom of the screen, so you open it on your phone or laptop, paste your notes and press Save. Then the TV picks them up within a few seconds and starts from the top.
- You can paste plain text or Markdown, so headings, bold, italic, lists, quotes and divider lines all show up nicely.
- It works with the remote you have. OK plays and pauses, the up and down arrows move through the text, and left and right change the size while paused or the speed while playing. Also, rewind and fast-forward jump, and 0 goes back to the top.
- It also remembers your size, speed and position, and keeps the screen awake while you read.
- It takes care of OLED TVs. The reading marker fades when you don't press anything and paused text dims after 2 minutes. After 10 minutes the TV can go to its screensaver, and the whole screen drifts a few pixels every minute.

### Install
- TV Prompter isn't on the Play Store and isn't from a registered developer (the APK is signed with my own key). That means you sideload it, and your TV or Play Protect may warn you about that. The [README](README.md#install) has the steps.
