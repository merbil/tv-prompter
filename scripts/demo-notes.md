# Why I stopped using to-do apps

Quick intro. Three apps in five years, and every one of them ended up as a graveyard of tasks I never did.

## The problem

- Adding a task feels like doing it
- The list only grows, it never gets shorter
- On Monday morning I spend 20 minutes *organizing* instead of working

> A list you never finish is just a nicer-looking worry.

## What I do now

One paper card per day, with **three things** on it. If it doesn't fit on the card, it doesn't happen today.

1. Write the card the evening before
2. Do the hardest one first
3. Throw the card away at night
